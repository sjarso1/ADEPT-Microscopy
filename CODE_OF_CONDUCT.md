# Code of Conduct

ADEPT is a collaboration between clinicians, engineers and students across
Uganda and the United States. We expect everyone who participates — in issues,
pull requests, meetings, and data-collection sessions — to be respectful,
constructive, and mindful that the ultimate users of this work are patients
and the pathologists who serve them.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discriminatory remarks, personal
attacks, and the disclosure of any patient-identifying information. Concerns
may be raised privately with the research advisor (Dr. Samson Jarso) or the
project sponsor (Dr. Tonny Okecha).

This document is adapted in spirit from the Contributor Covenant v2.1.
